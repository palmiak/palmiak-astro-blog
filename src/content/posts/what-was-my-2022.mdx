## Being a parent - the adventure continues

**Adventure** - this is the best word that describes being a parent. It's fun, unpredictable, and sometimes scary but great. I'm very glad we didn't have to sacrifice anything. Our lives changed, but we are all cycling together (cycling carriers are the best), traveling together, and going to cafes together.

We hope that 2023 will be similar but bigger, better, and more amazing.

## Doing live streams is fun

Last year I did 19 live streams for **Buddy**. I never, ever thought I would love it so much. Not only was it so fun, but I was also able to meet amazing people and learn a lot of interesting things (for example, I didn't know a thing about [SRE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R33UoHWVUqo) or [Crowd Testing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iMn0jFEyNfE)).

In fact, I liked it so much that I decided to start a channel of my own - [the Code and Coffee Show](https://codeandcoffeeshow.dev).

## New job

In October, I changed my job - I started working as a Dev Rel at [**Kinsta**](https://kinsta.com).**Kinsta** is mostly known as an amazing WordPress hosting company, but they launched Application and Database Hosting this year. This was the main reason I wanted to join them - having a chance to work more outside WordPress was very important to me.

After 3 months I can say it was a great decision and I'm very happy working there. I also had a chance to meet most of the marketing team in Athens.

## WordCamp Europe

Finally, WordCamp Europe happened. It was a true hugfest after the pandemic. It was also the first WordCamp our son attended (he was probably the youngest person there), and it was my first time leading a workshop session.

Was I stressed? Of course. Was it amazing an amazing feeling doing it? Yes, it was.

## Focusing less on WordPress

While working at Buddy, I started doing more stuff related to other CMSs and technologies. At first, it was a bit scary because I was always a WordPress person, but over time I liked it more and more. Right now, I really enjoy working with **Astro** and **Statamic**. It's a totally different experience than working with WordPress, but it's fun, and I feel I learned a lot.

I also created an article about the [World outside of WordPress](https://maciekpalmowski.dev/the-world-outside-of-wordpress/).

## Cycling around Tatry Mountains

With my friend Michał, we decided to cycle around the Tatry mountains - more than 300 kilometers in the Polish and Slovakian mountains. It was one heck of an adventure. There were moments when we thought we won't make it - especially while climbing up in the biggest heat. 

This was really something crazy, and I can't wait to repeat something like this next year.

Here you can read a [more detailed article](https://maciekpalmowski.dev/cycling-around-the-tatry-mountains/) about this trip.
