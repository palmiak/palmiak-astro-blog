In this article I will focus on connecting both CMSs together, we won't be touching any frontend though.

## What is HygraphCMS?

Hygraph describes itself as a _federated content platform represents the next generation of content management, going a step beyond being a headless CMS, helping you power a many-to-many relationship between different content sources and devices._

So, in short, it's a headless, GraphQL CMS that allows you to create content there or use 3rd party APIs.
